1. Jobs on the project record (5 min)

Objective. A Genesys job is a standard NetSuite project under the general contractor, carrying the contract value and the PM's estimate that drive WIP.

1.1 The job list Jobs Native

Show: Jobs list: the twelve jobs under four general contractors, with Contract Value, Estimated Total Cost and Job State as columns.

Each construction job is a project under the general contractor it works for, with its contract value and estimate right on the list. When a job is won in HubSpot, the award comes across with contract value, projected costs and resources, and accounting sets the project up from a template.

1.2 Contract value and estimate on the project WIP inputs Built

Show: Open 01-00177 Orlando Airport Terminal C: customer (Summit Ridge GC), status In Progress, start date, Contract Value $4,200,000, Estimated Total Cost $3,530,000, Include on WIP Schedule.

Two numbers drive the WIP: the contract value, including approved change orders, and the project manager's current estimate of total cost at completion. They sit on the project record and the PM updates the estimate. Everything else — cost to date, billings to date — comes from transactions, so nobody re-keys it into a spreadsheet.

Capabilities to name: Project records; project templates; customer-job hierarchy; project status.

2. Cost and billing land on the job (8 min)

Objective. Ordinary transactions put cost and billing on the job: vendor bills, the labour allocation and pay applications.

2.1 Materials bill coded to the job Job cost Native

Show: New bill from Westline Electrical Supply: Expenses line, account GI Job Cost - Materials, amount 180,000, Customer = 01-00177, memo Terminal C cable tray and Cat6A. Save.

A $180,000 materials invoice from Westline arrives. AP codes the line to the job — that one field is all it takes for the cost to land on job 01-00177. Purchase orders work the same way when you buy against a job.

2.2 Field labour from ClockShark and ADP Labour cost Native

Show: Open the labour allocation journal: one debit line per job to GI Job Cost - Field Labor, credit payroll clearing.

ClockShark stays for time. Hours by job times the cost rate from ADP payroll become one labour allocation per pay period, with a line per job. In implementation this is an import or a small integration rather than typing.

2.3 Pay application on the job Billing Native

The pay application has been approved in Textura. The amount billed to the general contractor is recorded on the job as an invoice, so billings to date are always in NetSuite.

3. The WIP schedule (14 min)

Objective. Genesys' WIP schedule, column for column with their Excel template, produced live from the projects and the GL.

3.1 Their template, in NetSuite WIP template Built

Show: Open Genesys Impact - WIP Schedule. Walk the column groups: Contract Totals, From Inception, Current Year, Prior Years; then the tiles.

This is the schedule you build in Excel today, with the same columns in the same order. Percent complete is cost-to-cost: cost to date over the PM's estimate. Earned revenue is that percentage times the contract. Compare earned with billed and you get the over and under billing. The current-year and prior-year columns split the same numbers by year. Across the twelve jobs: $19.7 million of contract, $13.3 million earned, $12.6 million billed, about $1 million under-billed and $310 thousand over-billed — before what we just entered.

3.2 What we just entered Live update Built

Show: Click Refresh. 01-00177 goes from 18.0% to 23.1% complete and its under-billing from $335.5K to $549.7K. On 01-00152 under-billing drops from $356.3K to $6.3K.

The bill we just entered moved Orlando from 18 to 23 percent complete, and because we haven't billed for that work yet, under-billing went up by about $214 thousand. The pay app on Miami Medical caught billing up to the work — under-billing there is down to $6 thousand. No spreadsheet, no month-end refresh.

3.3 Drill to the source Audit trail Built

Show: Click the JTD Contract Costs figure on 01-00177, then open the Westline bill from the list. Back, then click JTD Billings.

Every number on the schedule goes back to the bills, labour journals and pay apps behind it, so the auditors' question 'where does this come from' is answered with one click.

3.4 Loss job Loss provision Built

Show: The red row: 01-00155 Charlotte HQ — estimated cost $1.52M on a $1.45M contract; the note under the table.

Charlotte has been re-estimated above its contract value, so the schedule flags it as a loss job: a projected loss of $70 thousand. Under the revenue standard the whole expected loss is recognised now, not as the work is done, and the schedule makes sure nobody misses it.

3.5 The PM revises an estimate Re-forecast Native

Show: Open 01-00170 Capitol Hill Annex, Edit, change Estimated Total Cost from 905,000 to 1,010,000, Save. Back on the schedule, Refresh.

The PM tells us the Capitol Hill job will cost more than planned. They change one number on the project. Percent complete drops from 75 to 67 percent, earned revenue comes down, and job-to-date gross profit falls from $161 thousand to $74 thousand. The forecast change flows straight through to revenue and margin.

3.6 Year-end view As of date Built

Show: Set As of to 12/31 last year, Refresh. Then set it back to today.

Any date works, so the year-end WIP your auditors and your bank see is the same report. At last year-end there were eight jobs under way, $6.9 million earned against $6.9 million billed.

3.7 Export to Excel Excel Built

Show: Click Export to Excel (CSV) and open the file.

If the bank or the PE firm wants the WIP as a spreadsheet, it's one click, with the same columns as today's file.

3.8 Book the over and under billing WIP journal Built

Show: Click Post WIP Adjustment JE. On the journal: debit Costs & Est. Earnings in Excess of Billings and credit revenue per under-billed job; debit revenue and credit Billings in Excess per over-billed job; Reversal Date = the next day.

Month end: one click books the contract asset and contract liability from the schedule, job by job, and reverses on the first of next month, so the balance sheet and the income statement agree with the WIP without a manual journal.

3.9 Project profitability Profitability Native

Show: On 01-00177: the Financial / profitability view of revenue and cost on the job.

Because cost and revenue post to the job, NetSuite's standard project profitability works on the same data. The WIP schedule is the accountant's view; this is the PM's view of the same job.

Capabilities to name: Cost-to-cost percent complete; over/under billing; contract asset and liability; reversing journals; project profitability; saved searches and Excel export.

Presenter notes. If the CFO consultant asks about prior years: the schedule calculates prior-year revenue from cost at last year-end and today's estimate. Excel carries last year's figures forward; in implementation we would snapshot the year-end WIP. The JE button posts once per date; Reset removes it.
